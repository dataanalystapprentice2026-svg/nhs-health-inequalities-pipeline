# NHS-health-inequalities-pipeline
End-to-end public health data pipeline using SQL, R Studio, and Power BI

Evaluating Health Inequalities: A Cross-Sectional Analysis of Diabetes Prevalence and Socioeconomic Deprivation across London Local Authorities.

Hypothesis : London boroughs with higher Index of Multiple Deprivation (IMD) scores exhibit significantly higher rates of recorded diabetes prevalence due to the impact of wider social determinants of health.
